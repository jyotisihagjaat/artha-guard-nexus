✳ ARTHA / LINK LENS
Your link assessment will appear here.

Do not open suspicious links to test them. Never enter passwords, OTPs or payment details on an unverified page.

⌁

What Link Lens checks

  • Lookalike / misleading domain patterns
  • Unusual URL shorteners and raw IP addresses
  • HTTPS presence (helpful, but not proof of trust)
  • Urgent login, KYC or reward-related URL words
A clean result never means “safe”. Verify the domain independently through the organisation's official app or website.

07 / FRAUD RESPONSE PLAYBOOK

If something
feels wrong,
act calmly.

A simple checklist for a suspected digital payment scam. Use official channels and keep records.

01
Stop further interaction

Do not share more details, approve collect requests or follow caller instructions.

02
Contact your bank / payment provider

Use the official app, website or number printed on your card. Ask them to secure the account or payment method.

03
Report promptly in India

For suspected cyber financial fraud, call 1930 as soon as possible and report at the official National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal.

04
Preserve evidence

Keep transaction IDs, timestamps, phone numbers, messages and screenshots. Do not post sensitive details publicly.

Open official cybercrime portal ↗

This is general awareness information, not legal or banking advice. In an emergency, contact your bank immediately.
